Caffeine works by blocking adenosine, a chemical that builds up while you're awake and is a big part of what makes you feel sleepy. That's why a coffee perks you up: the sleep pressure is still there, it's just harder to feel.

## Why it lingers

Your body clears caffeine slowly. For a typical adult, about half of a dose is still circulating around five hours later, and roughly a quarter after ten. The pace varies a lot from person to person.

A late cup may not stop you from falling asleep, but it can make your sleep lighter and more broken without you noticing.

- Coffee, tea, energy drinks, many soft drinks and dark chocolate all contain caffeine.
- Decaf still has a small amount.
- Sensitivity differs: some people feel an afternoon cup at midnight, while others notice very little.

## Using it well

- Enjoy it in the morning and early afternoon, when it helps most and costs least.
- Notice how much you're having. Needing more and more to get through the day is often a sign of sleep debt.
- If you're cutting back, do it gradually over a week or two to keep it comfortable.
